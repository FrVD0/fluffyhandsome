ATTACOMIX

Welcome to the home of ATTACOMIX: “Comix for the Cosmos!” Being, as it were, a banner for the peculiar madness of the artist known as Your Uncle Attaboy and any friends as he may make along the way.

▼

Here you’ll find some random selections from my work along with links that will take you places where you can see more. Engage more.

All of my comix work is hand-made. I work primarily in pen and ink. Currently my favorite pen is a Koh-I-Noor Rapidiograph, .50. I also like messing around with brushes. Most of my work is in black and white, but I am planning to introduce much more color.

While I make art with my bare hands, I turn to agentic AI tools (built on LLMs like Claude) for this site — I describe what I want, and it plans, writes, tests, and verifies the actual code. If you’re a purist, and my AI-use is a dealbreaker, I thank you for your time and I understand if you don’t care to follow my work.

Still with me? Yay! I hope you enjoy the samples here. For regular updates, please check out my Patreon. The weekly comic/newsletter is free, and if you join the paid tier you’ll gain access to a substantial archive going back to 2020, plus the indubitably dubious honor of being among the first to see each new release.
