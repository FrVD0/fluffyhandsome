How the work is made

All of my comix work is hand-made. I work primarily in pen and ink. Currently my favorite pen is a Koh-I-Noor Rapidograph, .50. I also like messing around with brushes. Most of my work is in black and white, but I am planning to introduce much more color.

Watch it happen

To see me in action, check out my YouTube channel. Currently, the focus there is silent process videos focused on my series ATTACOMIX IN(ner)SANITIES. Future iterations will carry sounds: music, voiceover, and who knows what else. And perhaps other ATTACOMIX series will find their way there.

How this site is made

While I make art with my bare hands, I turn to agentic AI tools (built on LLMs like Claude) for this site — I describe what I want, and it plans, writes, tests, and verifies the actual code.
